The 2025 GMC Sierra 2500HD SLE 4WD Crew Cab Standard Box is a powerful truck with a gray exterior, designed for both work and adventure. Equipped with a 4×4 drivetrain and an automatic transmission, it ensures a robust and reliable performance in diverse conditions.

It's outfitted with the SLE Preferred Equipment Group, offering a comfortable yet functional space, complete with air conditioning, power windows, and convenient running boards.
